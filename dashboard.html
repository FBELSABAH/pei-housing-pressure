OFFICIAL DATA / LOCAL QUESTIONS

PEI Housing Pressure Monitor

How have population, housing supply and covered urban rental markets changed?
Province-wide context and urban rental signals are shown separately.

Coverage alert: completions in this source stop at 2022. Later years are missing, never zero. This monitor cannot establish a current housing deficit.

01 / People added in PEI

Persons; January 1 to January 1. No incomplete calendar-year value.

02 / New housing in PEI

Calendar-year units. Starts ≠ completions; completions stop at 2022.

03 / Average two-bedroom rent

Nominal CAD/month; observed average, not asking rent or fixed-sample inflation.

04 / Rental vacancy

Percent; October survey. Privately initiated row/apartment structures of 3+ units.

Household-size sensitivity / historical years only

Population change ÷ assumed people per household − completions. Positive means illustrative household-equivalent growth exceeded completions under that assumption. This excludes existing vacancies, household composition changes, demolitions, conversions and unmet need.

Definitions, limitations and source provenance

Urban survey-area labels are not municipal boundaries. Provincial population is not allocated to Charlottetown. No rural rental figures are inferred. Vacancy and rent series share the selected row/apartment 3+ structure definition, but different bedroom coverage: rent is two-bedroom; vacancy is all covered units. DGUID values are retained from each publisher table; do not interpret their vintage strings as proof of constant boundaries. Historical revisions may change future runs.

Built for investigation and reproducible analysis. No claimed policy impact or causal conclusion. Downloadable data and methodology are in the project repository.
