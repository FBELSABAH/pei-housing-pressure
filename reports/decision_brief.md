# PEI Housing Pressure Monitor

## What the data shows
2025 surveyed two-bedroom average rent: Charlottetown $1,354; Summerside $1,241 per month. 2025 rental vacancy: Charlottetown 1.7%; Summerside 3.2%. These describe covered rental structures, not all housing. The selected completions series ends in 2022. The monitor explicitly blocks a current supply-balance conclusion.

## Where to investigate
Prioritize a review of rental availability and rent composition in the Charlottetown survey area, where the selected 2025 vacancy measure is lower. Ask whether changes reflect new units, different unit quality, or existing-tenancy changes before calling them rent inflation.

## Next decision
Obtain and reconcile the current CMHC provincial completions series before using the monitor for a 2023-2025 supply assessment. Collect household formation, demolitions/conversions and vacancy-universe evidence before estimating unmet housing need.

## What the data cannot establish
Population growth is not household growth. Scenarios divide annual population change by assumed household sizes of 2.0, 2.5 or 3.0 and subtract completions only for years where both exist. The resulting balance is illustrative, not an official shortage, projection or causal estimate. No scenario after 2022 is reported. Provincial figures are never joined to urban rental observations as if they share geography.
